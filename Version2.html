Notes from this version

In this version I checked the VPHS workbook and verified that SummaryData is the sheet best suited for analysis. The workbook contains mental health and wellbeing indicators including psychological distress and life satisfaction and socioeconomic indicators including food insecurity. I also began checking the SEIFA and LGA boundary datasets so that later versions can join the data by Local Government Area
